Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

DonumAI is a free, open-source student project run by Kannan Murugapandian. It is designed to collect as little data as possible. This page explains plainly what happens to the information you enter. Contact: kannan.mp@proton.me.

1. What you enter

The form asks for an optional nickname, grade, subject, topic, curriculum, scope, familiarity level, language, teaching style, optional interests, your chosen AI model, and your Google Gemini API key. DonumAI has no user accounts and no database. Our application uses this information only to build and send your requests and to display the result. It does not save a copy. Please use a nickname and do not enter personal, sensitive or identifying information about yourself or anyone else.

2. Your API key

The key field is masked. Your key is sent to our server over an encrypted connection, held only in memory for a single request to Google, and then discarded. This applies both when you click "Load available models" (we use your key to ask Google which models your key can use) and when you generate a lesson. Our code does not write the key to disk, a database, or our own logs, and it sets no cookies. You can verify this in the public source code. Because the key passes through a hosted server, we cannot control what the hosting provider records at network level, so please use a key with a spending limit and delete or rotate it if you are concerned.

3. Hosting and server logs

DonumAI is hosted on PythonAnywhere. Web hosts normally record technical data such as IP address, time, requested page, and browser type in server logs. These logs are managed by PythonAnywhere under its own privacy policy. We do not use them to identify or track you. Servers may be located outside your country, so data may be transferred internationally.

4. Google Gemini

Your API key is used to ask Google's Gemini API for the list of models available to you, and your lesson details are sent to Google using the same key. We ask Google not to store the lesson interaction (the store=false option), but Google processes requests under its own terms. If your key is on the free (unpaid) tier, Google's Gemini API terms may allow it to use prompts and responses to improve its products. See also Google's Privacy Policy. Do not enter anything you would not want Google to process.

5. Cookies and tracking

DonumAI sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising, or tracking tools. Pages load no fonts, scripts or media from third parties; links to GitHub and other sites only load if you click them.

6. Legal basis and retention

We process the information you submit only to provide the lesson you requested, and technical data only as needed to run and secure the service (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) and (f)). Our application retains nothing after the lesson is returned. Retention by PythonAnywhere and Google is set by those companies.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Singapore's PDPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or object to the use of your personal data, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Since our application keeps no copy of your data, there is usually nothing for us to retrieve, but you can email us and we will help where we reasonably can, including pointing you to PythonAnywhere or Google for data they hold.

8. Children

DonumAI is meant for learners. Anyone under the age of digital consent in their country (16 in many places) should use it only with a parent, guardian or teacher, and should not enter their real full name, school, address or contact details. A Google API key also requires meeting Google's own age requirements.

9. AI notice

Lessons are generated by AI (Google Gemini) and may be wrong. No decisions with legal or similarly significant effects are made about you.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
